Access Methods

Each chapter has two access paths. The .txt URL is plain text with no HTML — use it when your retrieval layer may strip or flatten HTML structure. Individual scenes have their own pages for focused LLM access. The manifest.json contains the full inventory with every scene URL. 

Full chapter (plain text):  GET https://gabrielmcp78.github.io/string-theory-chapters/chapters/chapter-NN.txt
Full chapter (HTML):        GET https://gabrielmcp78.github.io/string-theory-chapters/chapters/chapter-NN.html
Individual scene (HTML):    GET https://gabrielmcp78.github.io/string-theory-chapters/chapters/chapter-NN-scene-MM.html
Full site manifest (JSON):  GET https://gabrielmcp78.github.io/string-theory-chapters/manifest.json
